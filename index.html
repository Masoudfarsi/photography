    The fine print

    Shipping
    Within Canada only, included in the price.
    Payment
    By Interac e-Transfer only. Prices are listed before tax; applicable tax is added to your total. Your print goes into production once payment arrives.
    Sales
    All sales are final. Every print is made to order, so I can't accept returns or exchanges.
    Colour
    A print may look slightly different from your screen. Screens give off light while paper reflects it, so colour and brightness depend on your display and the room the print hangs in.
    Damage
    If your print arrives damaged, send me a photo within 7 days of delivery and I'll reprint it at no cost.
    Giving
    10% of every purchase goes to charities and non-profit causes.

    About

    Photography for me is more than just capturing images; it's a journey into the art of seeing and understanding the world beyond words. I realize that language often falls short of expressing the depth of what we experience visually.

    Photography offers a unique way to bridge that gap, allowing us to explore the unknown and share perspectives that transcend cultural and linguistic barriers. Through my work, I aim to contribute to this universal connection, using the power of imagery to bring people closer to themselves and others.

    Prints are made on fine art matte paper with a white border for framing, and ship flat and unframed. A care card comes with every print, and 10% of every purchase goes to charities and non-profit causes.
